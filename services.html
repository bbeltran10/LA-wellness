Our Services 

Our practice offered a varied selection of services to best help all our clients needs.  

one-on-one Therapy

One-on-one Therapy, the therapist and client establish a relationship based on trust and mutual respect. This bond allows the client to feel comfortable sharing personal experiences, thoughts, and emotions. The therapist, in turn, uses various therapeutic techniques to help the client understand and navigate their feelings. The process begins with an initial assessment, where the therapist gets to know the client, their background, and the issues they are seeking to address. Following this, the therapist will develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to the client’s unique needs. As sessions progress, the therapist will guide the client through discussions about their life, helping them to gain insights into their emotions, behaviors, and relationships. The goal of one-on-one therapy is to empower individuals to make positive changes in their lives.

Couples Therapy

Couples therapy is a form of psychotherapy aimed at helping couples improve their relationship. It involves working with a trained therapist to address issues such as communication problems, trust issues, and conflicts. The goal is to improve the overall health of the relationship through structured sessions and various therapeutic techniques.It provides a safe space for partners to communicate openly and honestly, learn effective conflict resolution strategies, and strengthen their emotional connection. Many couples find that therapy helps them understand each other better and work through issues that might otherwise lead to separation.

Group Therapy

Group therapy is a form of psychotherapy where multiple individuals meet with one or more therapists to address common issues or concerns. While group therapy can be schedualed for a new group of clients LA wellness has 2 pre-established groups that meet at designated times.

Addiction

Addiction is a complex and devastating condition that affects millions of individuals and their families worldwide. Addiction can severely affect mental health, relationships, and overall well-being, whether it's substance abuse, gambling, or technology dependency. However, there is hope. Through the compassionate guidance of addiction Therapy, individuals can embark on a transformative journey toward recovery and rediscover a life of meaning and fulfillment.

Trauma

Trauma therapy is a specialized form of therapy that helps individuals heal from the emotional, psychological, and physical effects of traumatic experiences. It aims to address the lingering impact of trauma, such as developing healthy coping mechanisms, managing symptoms, and regaining a sense of safety

Meditation classes

Meditation classes at LA Wellness are structured sessions designed to teach and guide individuals in the practice of meditation, a technique for calming the mind and promoting focus. These classes are ment to offer a supportive environment and instruction from experienced instructors, making meditation accessible and manageable for both beginners and experienced clients.
